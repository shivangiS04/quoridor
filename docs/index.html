Quoridor

A two-player strategy board game for Android.

Move your pawn to the opposite side of the 9×9 board before your opponent does. On your turn you can either step one square or place a wall to slow your opponent down, but you may never completely block their path.

What you can do

  • Play the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard, with no internet needed after sign-in.
  • Play friends online with a room code. Moves are checked on the server, so everyone plays fair.
  • Sign in with Google or play as a guest. Guests can link Google later and keep their stats.
  • See your stats and a leaderboard of the best online players.

Get the app

Quoridor is in testing. Download the latest Android build from the Releases page (Android 8.0 or newer).
